## Interview Questions

### Section A: Initial Experience

1. **Can you tell me about your first experience using the NILM system?**
   - What was your first impression?
   - What did you notice first?
   - Was it easy to get started?

2. **How did you learn to use the system?**
   - Did you need help?
   - Was the interface intuitive?
   - What was confusing?

3. **What was the most surprising thing about using the system?**
   - Positive surprises?
   - Negative surprises?

---

### Section B: Feature Usage and Preferences

4. **Which features do you use most often?**
   - Why these features?
   - What makes them useful?

5. **Which features do you rarely or never use?**
   - Why don't you use them?
   - Would you use them if they were different?

6. **What feature do you find most valuable?**
   - Why is it valuable?
   - How does it help you?

7. **What feature is missing that you would like to have?**
   - Why would this be helpful?
   - How would you use it?

---

### Section C: Real-Time Monitoring

8. **How do you use the real-time monitoring feature?**
   - When do you check it?
   - What information is most useful?
   - Is the update speed acceptable?

9. **Has seeing real-time consumption changed your behavior?**
   - Give examples
   - What actions did you take?

10. **Do you find the real-time data accurate?**
    - Any discrepancies noticed?
    - How do you verify accuracy?

---

### Section D: Appliance Identification

11. **How useful is the appliance identification feature?**
    - Do you check which appliances are ON/OFF?
    - Has it helped you understand consumption?

12. **How accurate do you think the appliance identification is?**
    - Any incorrect identifications?
    - Which appliances are identified correctly/incorrectly?

13. **Has appliance identification helped you save energy?**
    - How?
    - Examples?

---

### Section E: Historical Data and Reports

14. **How do you use the historical consumption data?**
    - Daily, weekly, or monthly views?
    - What patterns have you noticed?

15. **Are the charts and graphs helpful?**
    - Easy to understand?
    - What improvements would you suggest?

16. **Have you used the PDF report feature?**
    - If yes, how useful was it?
    - If no, why not?

---

### Section F: Cost and Billing

17. **How useful is the cost estimation feature?**
    - Is it accurate compared to your actual bill?
    - Does it help with budgeting?

18. **For Tenants: Has the system helped with understanding your energy costs?**
    - Is the cost allocation fair now?
    - Has it reduced disputes?

19. **For Landlords: Has the system helped with billing tenants?**
    - Is it easier to allocate costs?
    - Have tenant disputes decreased?

---

### Section G: Alerts and Notifications

20. **Have you received any alerts or notifications?**
    - If yes, were they helpful?
    - If no, would you want to receive them?

21. **What type of alerts would be most useful?**
    - High consumption alerts?
    - Appliance-specific alerts?
    - Cost threshold alerts?

---

### Section H: User Interface and Experience

22. **What do you think about the mobile app's design?**
    - Is it attractive?
    - Is it easy to navigate?
    - What would you change?

23. **Is the information displayed clearly?**
    - Easy to understand?
    - Too much or too little information?

24. **What was the most difficult part of using the system?**
    - Why was it difficult?
    - How could it be improved?

25. **What was the easiest part of using the system?**
    - Why was it easy?
    - What made it user-friendly?

---

### Section I: Impact and Benefits

26. **Has using this system changed your energy consumption?**
    - Increased awareness?
    - Changed behavior?
    - Reduced consumption?

27. **What is the biggest benefit you've experienced?**
    - Cost savings?
    - Better understanding?
    - Fair billing?
    - Other?

28. **Has the system helped you save money?**
    - If yes, approximately how much?
    - If no, do you think it will in the future?

29. **Would you continue using this system?**
    - Why or why not?
    - What would make you stop using it?

---

### Section J: Challenges and Problems

30. **What challenges have you faced while using the system?**
    - Technical issues?
    - Understanding how to use it?
    - Getting accurate data?

31. **Have you encountered any errors or bugs?**
    - If yes, describe them
    - How did you handle them?

32. **What problems need to be fixed?**
    - Critical issues?
    - Minor annoyances?

---

### Section K: Comparison and Recommendations

33. **How does this system compare to other energy monitoring methods you've used?**
    - Better or worse?
    - What's different?

34. **What would you recommend to improve the system?**
    - Features to add?
    - Features to remove?
    - Features to change?

35. **What would you tell someone who is considering using this system?**
    - Would you recommend it?
    - What would you say?

---

### Section L: Tenant-Specific Questions

36. **How has the system affected your relationship with your landlord?**
    - Better transparency?
    - Fewer disputes?
    - More trust?

37. **Do you feel the billing is now fair?**
    - Why or why not?
    - What would make it fairer?

38. **Would you be willing to pay more for electricity if you could see your actual consumption?**
    - Why or why not?

---

### Section M: Landlord-Specific Questions

39. **How has the system helped with property management?**
    - Easier billing?
    - Better tenant relations?
    - Cost management?

40. **Would you install this system in all your properties?**
    - Why or why not?
    - What would influence your decision?

41. **How much would you be willing to pay for this system?**
    - One-time cost?
    - Monthly subscription?
    - Per-unit cost?

---

### Section N: Closing Questions

42. **Is there anything else you'd like to share about your experience?**
    - Additional comments?
    - Suggestions?
    - Concerns?

43. **Do you have any questions for me?**
    - About the system?
    - About the research?

## Interview Administration Tips

1. **Create a Comfortable Environment**
   - Choose a quiet, private location
   - Ensure good lighting and seating
   - Offer water or refreshments

2. **Build Rapport**
   - Start with easy questions
   - Show genuine interest
   - Be empathetic and non-judgmental

3. **Active Listening**
   - Listen more than you talk
   - Ask follow-up questions
   - Clarify when needed

4. **Recording**
   - Ask permission before recording
   - Use audio recorder or notes
   - Transcribe within 24 hours

5. **Time Management**
   - Keep interview within 30 minutes
   - Prioritize important questions
   - Schedule follow-up if needed

6. **Data Analysis**
   - Code responses by theme
   - Identify patterns
   - Compare across interviews
